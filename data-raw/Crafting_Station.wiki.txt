==Summary==
*The Crafting Station is a {{H|Defensive Buildings|defensive building}} unlocked at the highest [[Town Hall]] level (currently level 18), which can be toggled between three different modes, each with their advantages and disadvantages. It is free and covers a 3x3 area of the base.
**Even if the Crafting Station has not chosen its defense yet, it is still treated as a "defensive building". This means that defense-targeting units like [[Giant]]s, [[Balloon]]s, and [[Golem]]s can attack it as their priority, and it can also soak up [[Seeking Shield]]s and [[Fireball]]s.
**Once the Crafting Station's crafted defense has been selected, it can no longer be switched back to its non-damaging building form.
*The Crafting Station can be toggled between one of three defenses. It costs no resources to choose a defense, and they may be freely swapped between at any time.
*Each defense has three different modules, which can have upgrade time and costs resources to upgrade each level.
**Modules are improvements of defenses that can be upgraded, up to level 10 each. Each module controls a certain aspect of the defense, such as hitpoints, damage, attack frequency or unique effects.
*Upgrading modules requires a free Builder. Only one module can be upgraded on a given Crafted Defense at a time, but multiple Crafted Defenses may have modules upgraded at the same time.
*Module upgrades behave identically to building upgrades in terms of how they are conducted; they award [[Experience]] upon completion, can be immediately finished with [[Gems]], applicable [[Magic Items]] can be used on them, and they will be discounted by Builder Boosts in [[Gold Pass]].
*Crafted Defenses will always defend the village, even while upgrading. They will also start at level 3 (level 1 for each of the three modules), and can be upgraded up to level 30 (with all modules at level 10).
*Crafted Defenses from the Crafting Station last until a crafting phase ends, and another set is released with each following phase. Each Crafting Phase lasts for four months, which means there will be three each year, which will contain different crafted defenses for each set.
*Once a new Town Hall level comes out, the Crafting Station will remain available to players at the previously highest Town Hall level until the end of the Crafting Phase, upon which the Crafting Station will be removed if players do not upgrade their Town Hall by then.
*Upgrading Crafted Defenses gives 8 {{Res|Sparky Stone}} per module level. This can be spent at the [[Fancy Shop]]. This was also retroactively awarded for previous module levels done prior to the November 2025 update.
**Leveling one Crafted Defense to the maximum gives a total of 216 {{Res|Sparky Stone}}.
**Leveling all current available three Crafted Defenses to the maximum gives a total of 648 {{Res|Sparky Stone}}.
*Any Crafted Defense takes 54 days to fully upgrade, given uninterrupted building and excluding any builder boosts or perks. The 3 modules of each Crafted Defense require 13 days and 6 hours, 18 days, and 22 days and 18 hours to complete, each duration is assigned once.
**In season 3, the module upgrade time is changed to 16 days and 16 hours, 18 days and 2 hours, and 19 days and 6 hours, balancing the duration of each module more evenly, but still totaling 54 days for each Crafted Defense. The earlier upgrades is made to be much shorter, allowing players to access the higher stats faster and makes pivoting to a different Crafted Defense less punishing, it also makes Sparky Stones earlier to earn if it is at the end of a season.
***In Season 4, the module upgrade times are changed to 20 days and 20 hours, 22 days and 15 hours, and 24 days and 2 hours, totaling 67 days and 13 hours for each Crafted Defense. The upgrade durations per module have been balanced to adjust for each Town Hall levels.

===Current Defenses===
*The following defenses are the currently available Crafted Defenses:
**The [[Hot Candle]] flings flame spurts at surrounding units and splits its attacks among nearby units similiar to [[Multi-Archer Tower]], however its number of targets drops as it melts over time.
**The [[Hero Hunter]] targets all ground and air units with a range of 9.5 tiles while effecting them down with a [[Poison Spell]] effect, it deals extra damage against Heroes.
**The [[Cake-A-Pult]] launches destructive cakes at ground and air units within a range of 3-12 tiles, it leaves a explosive surprise behind after dropping.
===Former Defenses===
*The following defenses are the previously available Crafted Defenses:
**The [[Hook Tower]] grabs a unit into its blind spot with a range of 4-15 tiles, ground or air, but does not deal any damage and stuns them temporarily.
**The [[Flame Spinner]] is always active and deals area damage, with a range of 8 tiles. When attacking, it attacks in a spiral motion, releasing a large number of fireballs.
**The [[Crusher Mortar]] can deal heavy damage to ground units with splash damage with a range of 2.5-9 tiles. When attacking, it launches spiky, heavy stones rapidly at the opponent, and each bounces up to two times before disintegrating, similar to a [[Super Bowler]].
**The [[Hero Bell]] boosts defending Heroes while active, but does not deal any damage.
**The [[Bomb Hive]] is always active and launches bombs in set directions that can travel long distances and deal significant area damage.
**The [[Light Beam]] is a single-target defense with very long range, however its shots diminish in power the further away its target is.
**The {{H|Roaster}} fires bursts of plasma, dealing rapid splash damage against ground and air troops at medium range.
**The {{H|Air Bombs}} launches aerial bomb balloons, dealing high splash damage against air units.
**The {{H|Lava Launcher}} launches fiery projectiles at long range, dealing high splash damage against ground units. The projectiles leave lava pools on impact, further damaging ground units that enter them.

==Trivia==
*Each Crafting Phase was announced to be every three months, due to player backlash with the amount of upgrade and time required, Supercell extended the first Phase to last until Town Hall 18 released which lasted for around five months, and extended each Phase to last for four months each instead.
*Given it is treated as an defensive building, it can be placed outside the base to surprise the attacker and prevent significant value of the [[Fireball]] in a [[Grand Warden|Warden]] Charge.
