==Summary==
*The Hero Hunter is a Crafted Defense that can be chosen from the [[Crafting Station]].
*The Hero Hunter launches poisonous cards from a cannon, dealing single-target damage against [[Army|ground and air units]]. Units that are hit are poisoned and will be slowed down and take extra poison damage, as if they were afflicted by a [[Poison Spell]].
**Like poison from a [[Headhunter]], the poison damage wears off quickly over time, making it negligible as compared to card damage.
*[[Heroes]] take double damage from the cards, and they will be prioritised if possible (though they will not be targeted immediately if the Hero Hunter is attacking another troop when a Hero enters its range).
*The three modules are hitpoints, damage and Poison Spell level.

==Trivia==
*The Hero Hunter shoots different card projectiles depending on its target. It fires a sword card when attacking regular troops, while using a larger crown card when attacking [[Heroes]].
*The Hero Hunter is referred to as "Headhunter Tower" in the game files.
