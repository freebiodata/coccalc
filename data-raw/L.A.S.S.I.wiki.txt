==Summary==
*L.A.S.S.I is the first [[Pets|pet]] unlocked from the [[Pet House]], and is automatically available once the Pet House has finished being built.
*{{PreferredTarget|Plural=Yes|ProperNoun=true|HeroRadius|Target Radius=2.5}}
*L.A.S.S.I is able to jump over {{H|Wall}}s to attack [[buildings]] and ground [[Army|troops]] behind it.

==Strategies==
===Offensive Strategies===
*L.A.S.S.I works great with the [[Royal Champion]] because they can support each other during an attack and both have the ability to jump over Walls.
*L.A.S.S.I is not a damage dealer and functions like a mini-tanker for the Hero. 
**This could make choosing L.A.S.S.I over [[Diggy]] for the Royal Champion a more viable option where the burrowing ability of Diggy can expose the Royal Champion to fire.
**Also, she can be an alternative when Diggy is under upgrade in the Pet House.
*She can also assist in funneling with the [[Barbarian King]] by taking out [[buildings]] behind Walls that the Barbarian King may have trouble reaching. However, this may put her in range of defenses that the Barbarian King may be out of range to tank for, which given her middling hit points, means she will fall rather quickly if this happens. To alleviate this, either use [[Wall Breaker]]s to get the King in, or use [[Freeze Spell]]s to stop the {{H|Defensive Buildings|defenses}}.
*Alternatively, if the Hero she was paired to dies before L.A.S.S.I, then she will mainly act as a mini-tank thanks to her relatively high HP.

===Defensive Strategies===
*L.A.S.S.I will not stray very far from her Hero. As a result, you could leave a gap between a layer of Walls and a building that you want to protect from L.A.S.S.I, and if she is paired to the Barbarian King or [[Archer Queen]], L.A.S.S.I will not go for said buildings unless the Hero is attacking the Wall. In the latter case, however, note that the Queen will still be able to attack the building.
*Her fast movement speed can be considered a weakness at times, as she is faster than any Hero (barring the use of speed-boosting equipment such as the Haste Vial), and thus may charge ahead over long distances, allowing for your defenses to hit L.A.S.S.I first.

==Trivia==
*The L.A.S.S.I's name derives from the Finnish name for "''Lawrence''", which in turn comes from the Latin name "''Laurentius''", meaning "''from Laurentum",'' a city near Rome.
*L.A.S.S.I's appearance is similar to the [[w:c:clashroyale:Mini_P.E.K.K.A|Mini P.E.K.K.A]] from ''[[wikipedia:Clash Royale|Clash Royale]]'' as both are similar to the [[P.E.K.K.A]], both are smaller than the P.E.K.K.A, and both have one eye shaped like a diamond.
*L.A.S.S.I is a female, as confirmed by the description, matching the [[P.E.K.K.A]] who has a similar appearance.
**A Mother's Day [https://twitter.com/ClashofClans/status/1391392008342630406 Tweet] by Supercell implies P.E.K.K.A is the owner of L.A.S.S.I.
*The paw prints visible on the [[Pet House]] are most likely from L.A.S.S.I.
*L.A.S.S.I is a hound according to a [https://twitter.com/ClashofClans/status/1392479927438659584 video] by Supercell.
*L.A.S.S.I is shown gazing at a butterfly in the [https://clashofclans.com/blog/game-updates/meet-the-hero-pets-1.html announcement post] which is a reference to a [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_PGs-lrzvt4&ab_channel=Godson-Gaming commercial] of ''Clash of Clans''.
*The L.A.S.S.I makes a similar sound to a [[Miner]] when she attacks.
*[[w:c:squad-busters:L.A.S.S.I|L.A.S.S.I]] is featured as a [[w:c:squad-busters:Monsters|monster]] in the game ''[[wikipedia:Squad Busters|Squad Busters]]''.

===Comparisons===
*L.A.S.S.I is the only [[Pets|Pet]] to be a robotic animal rather than an organic one.
*She is one of the three Pets that are a female, the other being the [[Phoenix]] and [[Sneezy]].
