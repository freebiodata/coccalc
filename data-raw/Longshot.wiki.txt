==Summary==
*The Longshot is one of the Guardians who is unlocked and is selected by default when players upgrade to [[Town Hall]] 18.
*The Longshot appears to be a female royal blue anthropomorphic entity with glowing blue eyes, four pastel blue patches on each of her arms, and has a golden wristband on each of her wrists. She wears stone armor reaching from her body to her legs, with a golden belt that has a spike at the center, which it resembles a castle, and at the left side, she has a dark stone plate with golden lines. On her head, she wears a helmet with a similar layout as her armor, except it has a metal band with golden rims and hexagonal stubs, and at the top, she has an antenna attached to her head, and has a crossbow without arms that resembles a cannon with an open slit at the top, with thick golden outlines and spikes. She uses her head to fire an explosive bolt from a very long range, by either stepping herself back or bobbing her head back. This allows her to snipe enemies from afar and can even outrange [[Flame Flinger]]s.
*When tapping her, she faces the player and punches her fists together three times, with the first two releasing some blue energy, and the last one generating a small blue shockwave. If she was on the Town Hall, she will then jump down to the bottom right side of the Town Hall.
*When defeated, she collapses to the front and then to the ground, then explodes, creating a large blue shockwave that deals heavy damage to all nearby enemy units, ground and air alike, but does not knock them back unlike the [[Giga Bomb]].
*When the Town Hall is destroyed while she is still standing, the Longshot will forcefully jump down immediately if no attacking troops enter her trigger range beforehand. This is common on attacks where maxed-level [[Fireball]]s with triple [[Earthquake Spell]]s are used to destroy the Town Hall, paired with the [[Giant Arrow]].

==Strategies==
===Defensive Strategies===
*The Longshot has a very long range, allowing her to outrange every attacking unit in the game. One of the strategies used is to place the Town Hall at the core, especially paired with [[Spell Tower|Invisibility and Rage Spell Towers]], so that when the main army enters the base, she will be in action.
*Since the Longshot deals splash damage, she can take out swarms easily, but her attack speed is too slow, which she would be overwhelmed by them. Protect her with splash defenses to keep them at bay.
**However, her death damage can also take them out, and damage higher-hitpoint units, including Heroes like the [[Grand Warden]].
**In addition, the Longshot can even snipe the [[Royal Champion]] in a Champion Charge, as her arrow deals damage to both her and the [[Spirit Fox]], dealing heavy damage to if not defeat either or both of them.
===Offensive Strategies===
*The Longshot has a slow attack speed, but she can defeat swarms with ease because of her high splash damage. Overwhelming her with medium to high-hitpoint units can defeat her, but the damage when destroyed may take them out, especially when there are traps like the [[Giga Bomb]] and {{H|Spring Trap}}s, unless if the attacker uses the [[Grand Warden]]'s ability beforehand.
*To prevent her from wreaking havoc from afar, the attacker can either use a [[Totem Spell]] to redirect her attacks away from the army, a [[Freeze Spell]] to stop her from attacking, an [[Invisibility Spell]] to protect units from being damaged. Avoid using [[Headhunter]]s however, as they will not target her, unless if there are no more [[Heroes]] remaining in the base. [[Poison Spell]] can be used, albeit less effective as it will decrease her attack speed and movement speed by 30% of their usual effects. 
*Avoid using Flame Flingers to target an area of the base where the Longshot can attack from her attack radius, else they will be severely damaged, or even get destroyed prematurely, unless if it is tanked by a high-hitpoint unit like a Totem from the Totem Spell.
*Do not use the Totem Spell while she is jumping over a wall, otherwise she will still lock onto her current target if it had not taken out already, especially important units like the [[Grand Warden]] in Warden Charges.

==Trivia==
*Prior to April 2026 update, if the Longshot dies while she is still on top of the Town Hall, she will stay in her idle state for a few seconds before despawning, while still causing an explosion beforehand. This video can be viewed [https://imgur.com/a/NW5wUON here].
*When the Poison Spell was able to affect Guardians, if the Longshot was on top of the Town Hall, she would easily get taken out. This was removed as it made attacking easier, but in a way that only when the Longshot will take damage if she already jumped down.

===Comparisons===
*The Longshot and the [[Flame Flinger]] have the longest range out of any troop in the game, both having a range of 11 tiles, exceeding the [[Super Minion]]'s long range attacks, not counting Hero Equipment.
**Before the January 12, 2026 update however, she had an attack range of 12 tiles, 1 tile more than the Flame Flinger's, which made her the unit with the longest range in the game.
*The Longshot and the [[Smasher]] are the first [[Town Hall/Guardians|Guardians]] to be released in the game.
*The Longshot is the first and currently the only Guardian in the game that is a female.
